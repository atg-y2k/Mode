# Bundled site data — sources & licenses

These files are generated by `scripts/build-site-data.mjs` (run `node scripts/build-site-data.mjs`
to rebuild) and are loaded lazily by `src/site/` so ZIP → zone lookups and invasive-plant checks
work offline.

## zones.json — USDA plant hardiness zone by ZIP code

- **Derived from the USDA/OSU PRISM 2023 map; not the official USDA map.**
- Source: the 2023 USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map GIS data by the PRISM Climate Group, Oregon State
  University (https://prism.oregonstate.edu/phzm/), as compiled per ZIP code by phzmapi.org
  (https://phzmapi.org/, `all.json`, code MIT-licensed).
- Fallback for ZIPs phzmapi.org drops (~219 PO-box and single-organisation ZIPs without a
  centroid, e.g. 02455, 06338, 569xx): the PRISM Climate Group's own ZIP-code tables of the 2023
  map (`phzm_us_zipcode_2023.csv`, `phzm_ak_…`, `phzm_hi_…`, `phzm_pr_…`, columns
  `zipcode,zone,trange,zonetitle`, https://prism.oregonstate.edu/phzm/). © Oregon State
  University; free to reproduce and redistribute under OSU's terms of use — the data are altered
  (reduced to one zone per ZIP), hence the disclaimer above.
- Transformations: kept the zone and the ZIP centroid only (`zips[zip] = [zone, lat, lon]`),
  coordinates rounded to 3 decimals (~110 m); ZIPs only the PRISM tables list are zone-only rows
  (`zips[zip] = [zone]`). Both sources agree on the zone of every ZIP they share.
- Caveat: a zone at the ZIP centroid can be off by a half zone in hilly areas and near coasts or
  large water bodies. Users can override it in Settings.

## invasives.json — introduced & invasive plants

- Source: Simpson, A., Fuller, P., Faccenda, K., Evenhuis, N., Matsunaga, J., and Bowser, M., 2022,
  *United States Register of Introduced and Invasive Species (US-RIIS)* (ver. 2.0, November 2022):
  U.S. Geological Survey data release, https://doi.org/10.5066/P9KFFTOD
  (https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/62d59ae5d34e87fffb2dda99).
- License: **USGS US-RIIS v2.0, CC0** (public domain). Per USGS, the data are provided "as is"; the
  register is national/regional (lower 48 states, Alaska, Hawaii), not state-level.
- Transformations: kingdom Plantae only; one row per scientific name with its common name, a status
  digit per locality (0 not listed · 1 established · 2 invasive · 3 widespread invasive) and the
  synonyms listed under "Other name(s)" in `taxonRemarks`.
